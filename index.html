Can you see it? homework for two

A holds the phone so B cannot see it and taps Draw. A plays the word silently for ten seconds. B says the family, then the word, they read on A’s body. A taps Reveal. Swap. If B could not read it, the change did not show — and a change the audience cannot see is useless.

How to use this

Two doors

The app opens on two doors. Emotional Vocabulary holds the words, the coach, the drills and the ranking. Dialogue rules holds the twelve rules. “← Home” at the top takes you back.

Words

Seven families is for the room: fear, anger, sadness, joy, want, status, surprise. Keep those seven names in your head. Open one and find the exact word underneath. Under each name you will see what a person in that family thinks, what they want to do, and where the feeling tends to go next.

Tap a word for how it can be said: the reflective form (I’m nervous), the verb where one exists (I resent you), and the spontaneous line a person actually says.

How you make me feel names the feeling by what the other person is doing to you (I feel ignored). Every one is about your partner, so the scene stays on the two of you.

Draw gives you a random word to enter a scene carrying. Play it before you show the line.

Coach

Put the phone on a chair, play a scene, and every so often it calls an action, an object, a discovery, a body state or a speech restriction that you take into the scene, spoken aloud so nobody has to read a screen. Nothing you do in Coach is recorded.

Practice

Practice holds the sixty-second naming drill, the ladder, and “Can you see it?” for two at home. Nothing you do in Practice is recorded.

Ranking

Ranking is homework: ladders of up to ten words from one family, for you to put the emotions in order from least intense to most intense by your own ear; when a ladder is full, the app shows where we placed each word.

Dialogue rules

Three groups, twelve rules, each with a line that breaks it (No) and a line that keeps it (Yes). The rule of the day sits at the top and opens its rule.

Physical states

Physical states are not emotions. They are ways in when nothing else has arrived.

Appearance

Automatic follows the phone’s own light or dark setting.

Put it on your home screen

iPhone — in Safari:

  1. Tap the ··· button beside the address bar (older iOS: the Share square-with-arrow at the bottom).
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Leave Open as Web App switched on. Tap Add.

Android — in Chrome:

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right).
  2. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app, then Install.

It then opens like any app and works with no signal. Only your finished ladders are saved, and only on this phone; add the app to your home screen so the phone does not clear them.

For the Coach

Volume up. Keep the screen on and the app in front: if the screen locks or you switch apps, the calls stop and you tap Start again. The app asks the phone to keep the screen awake while a scene runs; if your phone ignores that, set Auto-Lock to Never during class.
